Self-hosted MCP server · Kroger Public API

Let Claude build your grocery cart.

kroger-mcp is a self-hosted MCP server for the Kroger Public API. Claude finds your store, searches products with price, promo, stock and aisle, and adds what you pick to your cart.

Stores

Find stores by zip or coordinates, filter by chain and department, and save a default.

Products

AISLE03

Cart

Add items for pickup or delivery once you link your Kroger shopping account.

kroger_login · add_to_cart

AISLE04

Your server

A Go server in Docker, over Streamable HTTP, with optional OAuth on the endpoint.

amd64 · arm64 · GPL-3.0

Tools

Everything on the list.

Ten MCP tools, grouped the way you shop. Claude picks them; you just ask for groceries.

STORES

  • search_locationsFind stores by zip code, coordinates or id.
  • get_locationGet one store.
  • set_preferred_locationSave a default store.
  • list_chainsList chains, or look one up.
  • list_departmentsList departments, or look one up.

PRODUCTS

  • search_productsSearch by term, brand or product ids.
  • get_productGet one product by product id or UPC.

ACCOUNT

  • kroger_loginGet a single-use URL to link your shopping account.
  • kroger_auth_statusCheck the linked account and default store.

CART

  • add_to_cartAdd items to your cart by UPC.

It fills the cart. You check out.

Kroger's public API can only add to a cart. It can't read the cart, remove items, see past orders or place an order, so you finish on the store's website or app.

Ten minutes to your first cart.

You need Docker, a Kroger shopping account and a free Kroger developer app.

Set it up $ docker compose up -d
