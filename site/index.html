Let Claude build your grocery cart.

kroger-mcp is a self-hosted MCP server for the Kroger Public API. Claude finds your store, searches products with price, promo, stock and aisle, and adds what you pick to your cart at King Soopers, Kroger, Ralphs, Fred Meyer, Fry's, Smith's and other Kroger banners.

Set it up View on GitHub

What it looks like

You: Make the King Soopers near zipcode 12345 my store, then add a gallon of 2% milk, a dozen large eggs and sourdough bread to my cart for pickup.

Claude: (searches stores, saves your store, searches each item) I saved the nearest King Soopers as your store and found Kroger 2% Milk (1 gal, $3.49, aisle 12), Simple Truth Large Eggs (12 ct, $4.29, on sale) and Private Selection Sourdough ($3.99). Add all three?

You: Yes.

Claude: (adds to cart) Added. Open the King Soopers app or website to pick a pickup time and check out.

An illustrative example. Prices and products come live from Kroger for your store.

What you get

Stores

Find stores by zip or coordinates, filter by chain and department, and save a default.

Products

Search by term or brand. Scoped to your store, results add price, promos, stock and aisle.

Cart

Add items for pickup or delivery once you link your Kroger shopping account.

Self-hosted

A Go server in Docker, over Streamable HTTP, with optional OAuth on the endpoint.

Tools

ToolWhat it does
search_locationsFind stores by zip code, coordinates or id.
get_locationGet one store.
set_preferred_locationSave a default store.
list_chainsList chains, or look one up.
list_departmentsList departments, or look one up.
search_productsSearch by term, brand or product ids.
get_productGet one product by product id or UPC.
kroger_loginGet a single-use URL to link your shopping account.
kroger_auth_statusCheck the linked account and default store.
add_to_cartAdd items to your cart by UPC.
It can't check out. Kroger's public API can only add to a cart. It can't read the cart, remove items, see past orders or place an order, so you finish on the store's website or app.
